# Companion manuscript and supplementary information

The repository's synthetic alcohol-oxidation benchmark is documented in the following companion materials.

## Main manuscript

Pennada, Gagan Sai Amith. *An Interpretable Chemoinformatics Benchmark for Evaluating Machine Learning Frameworks, Decoy Sensitivity, and Green Catalyst Prioritization.* Manuscript. School of Computer Science and Engineering, VIT-AP University, India. Supplied 4 November 2026.

## Supplementary information

Pennada, Gagan Sai Amith. *Supplementary Information: An Interpretable Chemoinformatics Benchmark for Evaluating Machine Learning Frameworks, Decoy Sensitivity, and Green Catalyst Prioritization.* VIT-AP University, India. Supplied 4 November 2026.

## Supporting data cited in the manuscript

- `synthetic_benchmark_dataset.csv`: Seed 42 reference data (5,000 synthetic reactions; 13 columns).
- `20_seed_metrics_log.csv`: per-seed model metrics and TreeSHAP summary for seeds 42–61.
- `supplementary_experiments_log.csv`: positive-control GroupKFold/random-CV and learning-curve results, as described in SI section S3.
